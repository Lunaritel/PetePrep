4. ACCURACY
Questions, answers, and explanations are read automatically from your PDF. The reading

DECAPete has no accounts and collects no data. The only time it uses the internet is
to check for a newer version of itself, and only when you press "Check for updates" or
turn on automatic checks in Settings.
